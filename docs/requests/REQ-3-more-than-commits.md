# REQ-3: More ways to stand out than commit count

Status: Accepted

## Summary

The owner wants several rankings instead of one commit count: contributions of the year, current streak, longest streak, merged pull requests in other people's repositories, and stars (D-3). Commit counts favor one style of work.

Last change: 2026-11-26, accepted by the owner into its slice

## Who asked and when
The owner, 2026-11-25, TikTok LIVE session where podio started.

## What they said
> (Paraphrase, no verbatim quote was recorded.) Commit count alone is not enough; there should be more ways to stand out.

## The problem behind it
A commit total says little about who contributes to other people's work and is easy to inflate. Streaks and yearly totals can still be inflated; this is accepted in v1 and stated on the page (D-9).

## Evidence
Unknown. No measurement of how much a commit total misleads.

## Decision
Accepted by the owner, 2026-11-26, into slice `ranking`.
